About Us

A trusted bridge between global brands and Bangladesh's finest manufacturers.

2026 Company Profile — Your Trusted Global Partner

Attire Edge Sourcing Limited is a Bangladesh-based apparel sourcing company committed to delivering high-quality garments to global markets. We serve as a bridge between global fashion brands and the finest manufacturers in Bangladesh.

From design concept to final shipment, our team ensures every step of the sourcing process is streamlined, transparent, and driven by quality — backed by a deep understanding of global fashion trends, sustainable sourcing practices, and ethical compliance.
